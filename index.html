About Rockstok

A live covers band built for good nights.

Rockstok is a live covers band with a set built around songs people know, love, and can sing along to. The site is styled around the aqua, smoky, magical cauldron look from the Rockstok logo while keeping everything clean enough for bookings and merch.

Use this page as the main band hub: upcoming shows, band info, booking contact, social links, and a Fourthwall-powered merch section all in one place.
