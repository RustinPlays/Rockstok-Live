About the band

High-energy rock for stages across the region.

Rockstok is an Auckland-based rock band bringing a high-energy mix of classic covers, modern favourites, and original music to stages across the region.

With a setlist spanning artists like Dragon, The Cars, Green Day, My Chemical Romance, Hello Sailor, The Cure, Beastie Boys, R.E.M., Pink Floyd and more, Rockstok puts their own punchy live feel into every song while still honouring the tracks people know and love.

Alongside their crowd-favourite covers, the band is now introducing original songs into their shows, adding a new edge to their sound and giving audiences something fresh to experience.

From pubs and private events to corporate functions, parties and weddings, Rockstok brings the spirit of rock and roll to every performance.
