Lighting hire FAQ

Useful things to know.

Every venue is different, so Justin will confirm the final setup requirements with your quote.

Can I book lighting without booking the band?

Yes. Every lighting package can be hired independently for parties, weddings, venues and other events.

What is the difference between Auto Mode and Live Operated?

Auto Mode is installed, programmed and tested before running automatically or reacting to sound. Live Operated includes a dedicated operator creating looks, movement and colour changes throughout the event.

Can haze be used at every venue?

No. Haze is used only where the venue permits it and its alarm requirements allow it. The package still works without haze, although beams are less visible in the air.

What access and power does the setup need?

I’ll need safe setup access, suitable power and enough room for the selected stands or truss. Send me the venue details and I’ll confirm what is required.

How long does setup and packdown take?

Timing depends on the package, room access and venue restrictions. Setup and packdown times are confirmed before the event so they can fit the venue schedule.

Can the lighting be used outdoors?

Outdoor jobs are considered case by case. Equipment must be protected from rain, moisture and unsafe wind, with suitable covered space and power available.

Are delivery and travel included?

Your quote will confirm delivery, travel and any access-related costs based on the venue location and selected package.

Can you create a custom lighting package?

Yes. If the standard packages do not suit the room, guest count or atmosphere, send the event details and Justin can recommend a custom setup.
