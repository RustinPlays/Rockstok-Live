About the band

High-energy rock for stages across the region.

Rockstok is an Auckland-based rock band bringing a high-energy mix of classic covers, modern favourites, and original music to stages across the region.

With a setlist spanning artists like Dragon, The Cars, Green Day, My Chemical Romance, Hello Sailor, The Cure, Beastie Boys, R.E.M., Pink Floyd and more, Rockstok puts their own punchy live feel into every song while still honouring the tracks people know and love.

Alongside their crowd-favourite covers, the band is now introducing original songs into their shows, adding a new edge to their sound and giving audiences something fresh to experience.

From pubs and private events to corporate functions, parties and weddings, Rockstok brings the spirit of rock and roll to every performance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Planning an event? Here are the practical details that help make the night run smoothly.

How much space does the band need?

A stage or performance area around 5 metres wide by 4 metres deep is ideal for the band, but we can often work with smaller spaces. We also need a small front-of-house area out infront of the stage for the sound desk. Send us the venue layout and we can confirm what will work.

Does Rockstok supply PA and lighting?

Yes. Rockstok can provide a PA system and stage lighting suitable for many venues and private events, and our dedicated sound technician is included with the band. If the venue already has production equipment, we can coordinate with them to confirm it meets the show's needs.

How long does setup take?

Allow minimum two hours for load-in, setup and soundcheck. Larger venues or more involved production may need extra time, which we will confirm before the event.

Can the band play outdoors?

Yes, provided there is a safe, level and fully covered performance area, reliable power and protection from wind and rain. Outdoor bookings should also have a wet-weather plan.

Can clients request songs?

Absolutely. Requests made well before the event are welcome, and we will let you know what can be added to the set. The final song choices depend on suitability and preparation time.

How many sets do you perform?

A typical booking is three 50-minute sets. We can tailor the running order and set lengths to suit the event schedule.

Do you travel outside Auckland?

Yes. Rockstok can travel beyond Auckland, with travel costs and accommodation added where required. Tell us the location when enquiring and we will include the details in the quote.

What power or stage requirements are needed?

We need a safe, stable performance area for the band, suitable dedicated power close to the stage, and a powered front-of-house area in the main room where our sound technician can set up the sound desk. We will confirm the exact power, access and production requirements with you or the venue before the event.

What happens during set breaks?

Background music will play through the PA during breaks so the atmosphere keeps moving. We can use your event playlist or coordinate a playlist with the venue or organiser.

What is included in the standard Rockstok package?

The standard Rockstok package includes the four-piece band, our dedicated sound technician, a PA system and a basic lighting rig. If the gig requires a larger or more specialised lighting setup, additional equipment can be hired and the extra lighting costs will be added to the quote.
